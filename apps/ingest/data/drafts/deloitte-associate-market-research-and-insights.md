## About the Role

Conduct secondary research using proprietary and public sources, assist in primary research activities by designing questionnaires and monitoring fieldwork, prepare raw data for analysis, and generate actionable business insights.

## What You Need

- Database Expertise
- Survey Platforms (Confirmit, Qualtrics)
- Data Tools (Tableau, Microsoft Power BI, Qlik Sense, R, Python)
- MS Office (PowerPoint, Excel, Word, Outlook)
- Shift Flexibility: Must be comfortable working the 11 AM to 8 PM shift
- Core Technical & Research Skills

## What You'll Do

- Secondary Research: Conduct in-depth research using proprietary and public sources (like Factiva, OneSource, Bloomberg)
- Primary Research Support: Assist in primary research activities by designing questionnaires, programming/testing surveys (using Qualtrics/Confirmit), monitoring fieldwork, and sharing interim findings
- Data Visualization & Analytics: Prepare raw data for analysis and develop highly visual, client-ready outputs using tools such as Tableau, Microsoft Power BI, Qlik Sense, R, or Python .
- Insight Generation: Analyze qualitative and quantitative data to develop comprehensive reports, point-of-view materials, thought leadership content, and actionable business insights.
- Project Management: Manage multiple research and analytics assignments simultaneously while collaborating with Deloitte practitioners to deliver work aligned with strict quality and confidentiality expectations.
